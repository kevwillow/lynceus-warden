{# The dead-man's switch, asked on the ntfy step rather than on one of its own. ⛔ It is NOT a separate wizard step, and that is deliberate. The heartbeat is delivered over ntfy, so it belongs beside the delivery path it depends on -- and step 7 already redirects straight to step 10 when the ntfy URL is empty, so an operator who skipped ntfy is never shown a question about a channel they do not have. A step of its own would have to re-derive that condition, and would renumber every step after it. Included by all three branches of ntfy_probe.html (skipped / ok / failed), because the question is worth asking whatever the probe returned: a failed probe is exactly when an operator most wants to know the delivery path is being exercised daily. #}
One low-priority notification every 24 hours. Lynceus default is OFF.
